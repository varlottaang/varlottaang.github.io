Hi, I'm **Angelo Varlotta**. I'm a project manager and consultant. I work at the intersection of machine learning, responsible AI, and sustainability. My focus is on making AI systems more thoughtful, fair, and ecologically aware.

I volunteer at [Neuromatch](https://neuromatch.io), an open-science organization that runs accessible, high-quality computational neuroscience and deep learning courses for researchers worldwide.

I've contributed science writing to [Ask A Biologist](https://askabiologist.asu.edu) at Arizona State University, translating scientific concepts into Italian for broader audiences.

## Interests

- Applied machine learning and its societal impact
- Responsible and ethical AI development
- Environmental sustainability in technology
- Open science and accessible education
- Science communication across languages
