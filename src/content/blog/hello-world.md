Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I've been meaning to set up a proper home on the web for a while. Something simpler than a social media profile, more permanent than a newsletter issue, and mine to keep. This is it.

## What I'll write about

My work sits at the intersection of a few areas I find genuinely interesting:

- **Applied machine learning** — the gap between ML research and real-world deployment is wide and fascinating. I'm interested in what actually works, at scale, in messy environments.
- **Responsible AI** — not as a compliance checklist, but as a design philosophy. Who benefits? Who bears the risk? Who was in the room when the system was built?
- **Sustainability** — AI systems have a physical footprint. Data centres consume water and energy. This is worth thinking about carefully.

I also do science communication in Italian, which opens up a different kind of challenge: translating not just words, but intuitions.

## Neuromatch

I volunteer with [Neuromatch](https://neuromatch.io), which runs open, accessible courses in computational neuroscience and deep learning. It's one of the better examples I've seen of what global, community-driven science education can look like. I'll write more about that here too.
